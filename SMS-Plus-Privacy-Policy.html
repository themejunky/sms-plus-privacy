Privacy Policy

App: Dual Sim SMS Messenger 2020 / SMS Plus
Developer: APLICATII ANDROID PLUS SRL
Package names: com.smsplus.app, com.aplicatiiandroidplus.smsplus
Last updated: June 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Dual Sim SMS Messenger 2020 / SMS Plus ("SMS Plus", "the App", "we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use the App. SMS Plus is an Android SMS/MMS messaging and inbox customization app. The App is designed to process your messages and related content on your device.

Summary

SMS Plus does not sell your personal information. The App does not upload your SMS, MMS, contacts, message content, message attachments, call logs, or location to our servers. Message and contact data is used locally on your device so the App can provide SMS/MMS messaging, inbox display, search, notifications, conversation management, and customization features. Some store versions may offer optional subscriptions or purchases to unlock premium themes, live effects, or related customization features.

Information The App Can Access On Your Device

Depending on the features you use and the permissions you grant, SMS Plus may access the following types of data on your device:

  • SMS and MMS messages: used to display your inbox, send and receive messages, manage conversations, show notifications, and maintain local message history.
  • Contacts: used to display contact names and photos, choose recipients, and help you attach or share contact information when you explicitly request it.
  • Phone state and SIM information: used for messaging behavior, dual-SIM support, and compatibility with Android telephony features.
  • Photos, media, files, camera, microphone, and storage: used only when you choose to attach, capture, import, export, back up, restore, or share media or files through the App.
  • Location: used only if you choose a feature that shares or attaches your location in a message.
  • Notification and device settings: used to provide message notifications, badges, vibration, and related messaging features.

How Information Is Used

The App uses information available on your device to provide core functionality, including:

  • displaying and organizing SMS/MMS conversations;
  • sending and receiving SMS/MMS messages through Android and your mobile carrier;
  • showing sender names, contact photos, message notifications, and unread counts;
  • supporting attachments such as images, contacts, audio, and location when you choose to use those features;
  • customizing the appearance of the inbox, conversation screen, themes, colors, and related UI elements;
  • importing, exporting, restoring, or maintaining local message data when you initiate those actions.

Data Storage And Retention

SMS Plus stores message and customization data locally on your device as needed for the App to function. The App does not maintain a separate cloud copy of your SMS/MMS messages on our servers. Data remains on your device until you delete it, clear the App data, uninstall the App, or use Android/system tools to remove it.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We do not share your SMS, MMS, contacts, message content, message attachments, call logs, or location with advertisers or data brokers.

Information may leave your device only in limited cases controlled by you or required for normal device operation, for example:

  • when you send an SMS/MMS message, the message and selected attachments are transmitted through Android, your mobile carrier, and the recipient network;
  • when you choose to share a contact, file, photo, audio recording, or location in a message, that selected content is sent to the recipient you choose;
  • when the App downloads themes, stickers, GIFs, or other optional online content, the request may be processed by the content provider or hosting service. Those requests may include standard technical information such as IP address, device/browser request metadata, and the requested URL;
  • when required by law, legal process, or enforceable governmental request.

Advertising, Analytics, And In-App Purchases

SMS Plus does not sell user data for advertising. Store-specific releases may differ: the Samsung Galaxy Store version currently disables third-party advertising and may use Samsung In-App Purchase to process optional premium subscriptions; Google Play releases may use Google Play Billing and approved advertising SDKs when those features are enabled for that store.

Purchase processing is handled by the applicable app store payment provider, such as Samsung Galaxy Store or Google Play. SMS Plus may use purchase identifiers, product identifiers, subscription status, and receipt verification responses to unlock or restore premium access. We do not receive your full payment card details from the store provider.

If advertising, analytics, subscriptions, purchases, or third-party SDKs materially change in a future release, this Privacy Policy and the applicable store data-safety declarations will be updated before or at the time those features are released.

Permissions

SMS Plus requests Android permissions only to support messaging and customization features. You can manage App permissions in Android Settings. Some features may not work if required permissions are denied, including SMS/MMS access, contact display, media attachments, camera capture, audio recording, storage access, notifications, or optional location sharing.

Children's Privacy

SMS Plus is not directed to children under 13 and is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us so we can review the issue.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect the App and limit unnecessary data transfer. No mobile app or communication system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. SMS/MMS messages are also handled by Android, device manufacturers, mobile carriers, and recipient networks outside our control.

Your Choices

You can control your information by managing Android permissions, deleting messages, clearing App data, uninstalling the App, or using any backup/export/delete features available in the App or Android. For messages already sent to other people, deletion on your device does not remove copies from recipient devices or carrier systems.

Data Deletion Requests

SMS Plus does not create an app account. Most SMS Plus data, including SMS/MMS messages, contacts shown in conversations, media attachments, and customization data, is stored locally on your device. You can delete this local data by deleting messages in the App, clearing the App data in Android Settings, uninstalling the App, or using Android/system deletion tools.

To request deletion of app-related data that APLICATII ANDROID PLUS SRL may control outside your device, email contact@chrisbarin.com with the subject "SMS Plus data deletion request". Include the app name, package name, store where you installed the App, and any information needed to locate your request, such as the email address you used to contact support.

Data that may be deleted or anonymized includes support messages you send us and app-related analytics or crash diagnostic records associated with device or installation identifiers where technically available. Store payment records, subscription status, and receipts are handled by the applicable store provider, such as Samsung Galaxy Store or Google Play; we may retain limited purchase verification, fraud prevention, security, compliance, tax, accounting, or legal records for as long as required by law or legitimate operational need.

We review deletion requests and delete or anonymize data we control within 90 days unless retention is required for legal, security, fraud prevention, accounting, dispute resolution, or compliance reasons.

International Users

The App may be used in different countries. If you use online features such as theme, sticker, or GIF downloads, technical request data may be processed in countries where hosting providers or content services operate.

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App changes, when legal requirements change, or when we add, remove, or modify features. The updated version will be posted with a new "Last updated" date.
